# Balances, daily rewards, and leaderboards

## Balance and payments

Use `/bal` to view your balance. Use `/pay <player> <amount>` to send money to another player. You cannot pay yourself, and a payment that fails validation does not move money.

The server sets the starting balance for new accounts and may set a maximum balance. EconomyCraft also records balance changes in your transaction history when transaction logging is enabled.

## Daily reward

Claim the daily reward with `/daily` or `/eco daily`. Each player can claim once per day. The amount is set by the server. If a reward cannot be added to your balance, contact a server administrator.

## Transaction history

Open `/transactions` or `/eco transactions` to review your recent balance activity. The server controls whether transaction logging is enabled and how long log files are retained.

## Leaderboards

Open `/eco` and choose **Leaderboards** to see rankings such as top balances, earners, spenders, sellers, buyers, and traders. The balance sidebar can also be enabled by the server.

Wealth taxes and faction levies are fiscal adjustments; they are excluded from earned and spent leaderboard totals.
