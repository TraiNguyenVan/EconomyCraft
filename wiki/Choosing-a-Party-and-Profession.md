# Choosing a Party and Profession

You can choose two character tags: a **party** and a **profession**.

## Choosing your tags

1. Open the menu with `/tag`, or open `/eco` and select **Tags**. You can also open each menu directly with `/eco party` or `/eco job`.
2. Choose a party: *Communism*, *Capitalism*, *Monarchy*, or keep the default *Anarchism*.
3. Choose a profession: *Builder*, *Farmer*, *Miner*, *Merchant*, or *Soldier*.
4. Before applying a choice, the confirmation screen shows its details and warns about the 30-hour lockout. Select **Confirm** to activate the tag.

See [Factions](Factions) for party benefits and [Professions](Professions) for profession details.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **30-hour lockout:**
> After choosing a party or profession, that choice is locked for **30 real-time hours**.
> Party changes, including `/party leave`, are locked during this period. `/job leave` clears your profession and progress; after clearing it, you can choose a profession again. A normal profession switch through the menu is locked for 30 hours.

## Where tags appear

The system runs entirely on the server and works with a standard vanilla Minecraft client:

- **Player list (Tab list):** Hold <kbd>Tab</kbd> to see the full tag names in their respective colors, for example `[Communism][Builder] Player_Name`.
- **Above the character (nametag):** Small party and profession icons appear, for example `[☭][⚒] Player_Name`.
- **Chat:** Small icons appear before the messages you send.
- **View another player's tags:** Administrators can use `/tag <player_name>` to see that player's current party and profession.
