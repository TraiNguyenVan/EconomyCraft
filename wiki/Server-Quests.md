# Server quests

Server quests are requests funded by the server. Find current quests in `/orders`, marked as server-funded bounties. Fulfill a request through the normal orders interface to deliver the requested items and receive its stated reward, subject to any applicable tax.

The board is drawn from eligible items in the server's price catalog and refreshes by period. Each period has a server-funded budget; the board may stop posting once the budget is used. Open quests expire when a period rolls over and do not carry forward.

When quest buyback is enabled, items collected from completed quests can later appear as auction-house listings posted by the server. These listings are marked as quest buybacks, are tax-free to purchase, and cannot receive price offers.

The board and buyback market can be enabled or disabled by server administrators. They can also tune the period, budget, eligible item prices, and other quest settings.
